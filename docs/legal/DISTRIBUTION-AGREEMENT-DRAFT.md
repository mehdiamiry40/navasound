## Required agreement sections

1. **Appointment and licence** — non-exclusive, limited rights needed to encode,
   reproduce, deliver, monetize, promote, administer and remove the accepted release.
2. **Ownership** — artist retains masters; no publishing or neighbouring-rights grant
   unless a separate schedule expressly says so.
3. **Delivery and discretion** — metadata, asset and lead-time requirements; QC rights;
   no guarantee that a provider or DSP accepts or keeps content live.
4. **Artist warranties** — authority, ownership, contributor consents, samples, covers,
   artwork, names, likenesses, metadata accuracy, tax details and lawful content.
5. **Prohibited conduct** — infringement, impersonation, deceptive metadata, artificial
   streaming, guaranteed streams, paid playlist placement and attempts to evade review.
6. **Fees and consumer rights** — total price, work-start point, changes, refunds,
   Australian Consumer Law savings clause and chargeback handling.
7. **Royalties** — actual receipts basis, currencies, provider deductions, reserves,
   fraud adjustments, statements, payout threshold, payment method and timing.
8. **Taxes** — GST status, withholding, invoices and responsibility for artist taxes.
9. **Takedowns and updates** — request process, timeframes, costs, store delays and
   provider-initiated removals.
10. **Term and termination** — initial term, renewal, post-termination takedown, unpaid
    balances, data/export access and survival clauses.
11. **Claims and indemnity** — notice, cooperation, control of defence and proportionate
    responsibility, subject to legal review and non-excludable law.
12. **Privacy and data** — approved processors, overseas disclosure, security, retention,
    access and breach handling.
13. **Disputes** — internal escalation, good-faith negotiation, Queensland law and
    appropriate court jurisdiction.
14. **General** — notices, assignment, subcontracting, force majeure, severability,
    entire agreement and electronic acceptance.
