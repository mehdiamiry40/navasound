## Mandatory rejection conditions

- Unclear DSP delivery authority or royalty chain.
- No written fee schedule or unilateral undisclosed charges.
- No usable catalog export or post-termination takedown process.
- Weak KYC, rights review or artificial-streaming controls.
- Rights grab, unnecessary exclusivity or ownership transfer.
- Inability to explain reserves, deductions, statements and payout timing.
